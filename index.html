Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computing at the University of Connecticut, advised by Prof. Chuxu Zhang. Before that, I obtained my master's degree from Hunan University under the supervision of Prof. Youhuan Li, and my bachelor's degree from South China Normal University. I am deeply grateful to Prof. Youhuan Li, whose guidance allowed me to experience the fascinating world of scientific research.

Research Interests

I work on large language models, with a focus on LLM agents and post-training. I am interested in how agents decide when and how much to think, and how training can make such decisions reliable across environments. Before moving to LLMs, I worked on databases and graphs, including subgraph matching, graph query processing, and graph neural networks for database systems. If you are interested in collaboration, feel free to contact me.
